The suggested-changes report is a structured hand-off for your genealogy software: merge suggestions, missing dates, and unverified facts — with attribution and timestamps. It never edits the GED file itself.

Quick guide

  1. Choose a .ged — or click Load Sample Family to try everything safely.
  2. Parse GED — check the Diagnostics tab for data issues.
  3. Media Library — import your photo/document folders; they attach to people and families.
  4. Build — pick a privacy mode and export profile, then generate the explorer or vaults.
  5. Share the explorer HTML with family — they add memories in Tree Refinement Mode and export JSON backups.
  6. Refine tab — import all backups, review conflicts, and export one merged backup.
  7. Booklet tab — print a keepsake Family Story Booklet.

Privacy statement

Grandmothers voice by Kayock runs entirely on your device. It has no server, no account, no telemetry, and no network code. Your GED file, media, and notes never leave your computer unless you personally share the exported files. Refinement notes are saved in your browser’s local storage on this device only — export JSON backups to move them between computers. The original GED file is opened read-only and is never modified or overwritten.

Data safety

The imported GED is only read. Corrections, memories, and merge suggestions are exported as separate reports for human review. Living-person redaction is estimated from dates (deceased marker, or birth within the threshold) — always double-check before sharing.
